Clinical Disease Nutrition & Diet Guidelines

Low-Glycemic Diabetes Cafe Plan

Metabolic

Slow-release complex carbohydrates and high-fiber foods to stabilize insulin sensitivity.

  • Morning: Overnight soaked fenugreek (methi) water + sprouted moong salad
  • Lunch: Multigrain rotis (barley/chickpea) + bitter gourd (karela) + yellow dal
  • Evening Snack: Roasted makhana (foxnuts) or unsalted almonds
  • Dinner: Grilled paneer / tofu with sauteed broccoli and zucchini
Strictly Avoid: White sugar, sweetened teas, fruit juices, bakery maida, potatoes.

DASH Heart-Protective Cafe Plan

Cardio

Potassium-rich, ultra-low sodium meals to relieve arterial pressure and promote endothelial health.

  • Morning: Rolled oats with crushed walnuts, chia seeds and sliced banana
  • Lunch: Brown rice with garlic spinach (palak), boiled rajma beans and salad
  • Beverage: Fresh beetroot and pomegranate juice (natural dietary nitrates)
  • Dinner: Bottle gourd (lauki) soup with whole wheat toast
Strictly Avoid: Pickles, papad, processed canned meats, salted butter, deep-fried samosas.

Alkaline Gut Soothing Cafe Plan

Digestive

Non-acidic, mucosal protective foods that calm heartburn, gastritis, and peptic ulcers.

  • Morning: Ripe papaya slices with soaked sabja seeds and chilled tender coconut water
  • Lunch: Soft curd rice with fresh coriander and a pinch of roasted cumin
  • Evening Sip: Chilled skimmed buttermilk (chaas) with mint leaves
  • Dinner: Moong dal vegetable khichdi (soft and lightly salted)
Strictly Avoid: Spicy red chilies, vinegar, tomato ketchup, strong coffee, chocolate, citrus fruits.

Viral Recovery & Immunity Broth Plan

Immunity

High-electrolyte hydration and easily assimilable protein broths for fever and infection.

  • Morning: Warm ginger, tulsi, and turmeric herbal infusion with lemon-honey
  • Lunch: Clear vegetable lentil broth with soft boiled carrots and rice
  • All Day: Electral ORS hydration solution (1 sachet in 1 liter water)
  • Dinner: Steamed idlis with light vegetable stew or chicken broth
Strictly Avoid: Iced drinks, cold water from fridge, sour curd, street food, heavy oil.
